# [Consultation Guide] Device and Plan Policy
<!-- DOC_CHUNK_001: Overview and scope -->
## 1. General Principles
This document defines the standard commercial terms for consumer subscriptions, device subsidy allocation, the 180-day baseline plan commitment, and the difference penalty settlement policy.
The retail additional subsidy may be paid up to 15% of the public subsidy, and the net purchase principal equals the factory price minus the total subsidy (public subsidy + retail additional subsidy).

<!-- DOC_CHUNK_002: Baseline plan commitment -->
## 2. 180-Day Baseline Plan Commitment
1. Subscribers who receive a public subsidy must keep their initial plan at or above the designated baseline plan threshold (for example 5G 45,000 KRW/month or LTE 26,400 KRW/month) for **180 days** from the activation date.
2. If a subscriber downgrades below the baseline within 180 days, a difference penalty is calculated from the elapsed days and charged.

<!-- DOC_CHUNK_003: Penalty formula -->
## 3. Difference Penalty Formula
The difference penalty is calculated with the following deterministic formula:
`Penalty = (Initial Granted Subsidy - Lower Tier Subsidy) * (180 - Elapsed Days) / 180`
- If the elapsed days are 180 or more, the penalty is 0 KRW (commitment completed).
- Statutory exemption: downgrades to designated senior, youth or social welfare plans are exempt regardless of the elapsed days.
