## 4. Statutory Penalty Exemption Clauses
1. **Senior and social welfare exemption (`RULE_WELFARE_EXEMPT`)**: subscribers registered as verified seniors (age 65+) or social welfare recipients are exempt from the difference penalty (`0 KRW`) when they switch to a designated senior or welfare plan, even within the 180-day commitment window.
2. **Military enlistment hold (`RULE_MILITARY_HOLD`)**: when a line is suspended for active-duty enlistment, the 180-day commitment clock pauses for the verified service period and no early termination penalty is charged.

## 5. Public Subsidy vs. 25% Selective Contract (`RULE_TCO_COMPARE`)
1. **Public subsidy path**: `24M Total Cost = (Factory Price - Total Subsidy) + (Monthly Plan Fee * 24)`
2. **25% selective discount path**: `24M Total Cost = Factory Price + (Monthly Plan Fee * 0.75 * 24)`
3. **Consultation guideline**: agents compute both paths and recommend the option with the lower 24-month total cost of ownership (TCO), together with the exact savings.
