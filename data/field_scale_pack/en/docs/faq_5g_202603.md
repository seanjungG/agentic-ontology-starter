# Agent FAQ — 5G smartphone line (as of 2026-03)

> Document no.: FAQ-5G-202603 · Compiled: 2026-03-15 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> These answers were compiled as of 2026-03-15. Any policy amendment notice issued after that date prevails, and amounts in this FAQ may have changed.

**Q1. How much MNP bonus can an H-tier 5G phone port-in get?**
A. Up to KRW 300,000 at the moment. During the promotion we can add KRW 50,000.

**Q2. And on the M-tier?**
A. Up to KRW 200,000.

**Q3. How long must they have been with the old carrier?**
A. At least 90 days.

**Q4. What is the Fam pack discount for 3 lines?**
A. KRW 5,000 per line per month. 2 lines: KRW 3,000; 4 or more: KRW 7,000.

**Q5. What does a PhoneCare customer get for a lost device?**
A. The cover is 80% of the list price, of which 25% is the customer's excess.

**Q6. And a cracked screen?**
A. Repair is covered up to KRW 400,000 per incident and the excess is KRW 50,000.

**Q7. How much does the Return plan pay after one year?**
A. 55% of the list price; 30% after two years.

**Q8. How much is the Long-run discount in year 5?**
A. KRW 4,000 per month. Year 3: KRW 2,000; year 10: KRW 7,000.

**Q9. Until when can insurance be taken out?**
A. Within 30 days of activation.

**Q10. Can a selective-discount customer get the MNP bonus?**
A. Yes, but not the public subsidy or the retailer additional subsidy.
