## 2. Family bundle discount (2025 rules)

2 lines KRW 2,000, 3 lines KRW 3,000, 4 or more KRW 4,500 (monthly discount per line). It could not be combined with the selective discount.

## 3. Device insurance (2025 rules)

Loss coverage was 65% of the list price and the loss deductible rate 20%. Damage was covered up to KRW 220,000 per incident with a deductible of KRW 30,000.

## 4. Device trade-in reward (2025 rules)

35% after 12 months of use, 20% after 24 months.

## 5. Loyalty discount (2025 rules)

From 3 years KRW 1,000, from 5 years KRW 2,000, from 10 years KRW 4,000 (monthly discount).
