# Policy amendment notice 2026-07

> Document no.: NOTICE-2026-07 · Issued: 2026-06-22 · Effective: 2026-07-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> The switch bonus applies to lines **activated** on or after the effective date, device insurance to **incidents** on or after it, and the trade-in reward to devices **returned** on or after it. Family bundle and loyalty discounts apply to every line from the bill of the month of the effective date, whatever the activation date. Earlier cases follow the rules in force at the time.

## Item 1 (Port-in switch bonus — further adjustment)

The MID-tier limit amended by NOTICE-2026-06 item 7 is amended again **from KRW 190,000 to KRW 180,000**.

## Item 2 (Loyalty discount — further adjustment)

The discount from 10 years amended by NOTICE-2026-02 item 1 is amended again **from KRW 2,500 to KRW 3,000**.

## Item 3 (Family bundle discount — further adjustment)

The 2-line discount amended by NOTICE-2026-06 item 4 is amended again **from KRW 1,000 to KRW 500**.

## Item 4 (Switch bonus clawback method)

Article 4(1) of GUIDE-SWITCH-5G and GUIDE-SWITCH-LTE is amended as follows. The switch bonus guides of the other lines are not amended.

1. Cancelling or moving below the MID tier **before 90 days** from activation: clawback **in full**, as before.
2. **From 90 days and before 180 days**: only the remaining period is clawed back. Clawback = switch bonus received × (180 − days elapsed) ÷ 180 (rounded to the won).
3. From 180 days: no clawback (as before).

## Item 5 (Port-in switch bonus — adjustment)

## Item 6 (Device insurance — further adjustment)

## Item 7 (Port-in switch bonus — adjustment)

## Item 8 (Device insurance — further adjustment)
