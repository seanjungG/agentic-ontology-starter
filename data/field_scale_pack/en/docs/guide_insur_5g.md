# Device insurance guide — 5G smartphone line

> Document no.: GUIDE-INSUR-5G · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> Amounts, rates and periods in this document may be changed by later policy amendment notices (NOTICE). A notice prevails over this document.

## Article 1 (Enrolment)

1. Device insurance for the 5G smartphone line can be taken out only **within 30 days of activation**.
2. Insurance is per line; after a device change the new device must be enrolled again.

## Article 2 (Coverage)

1. **Loss**: the coverage limit is **80%** of the device list price on the day of the incident.
2. **Damage**: repair cost is covered up to **KRW 400,000** per incident.

## Article 3 (Deductible)

1. Loss: the customer bears **25%** of the coverage limit. Amount actually paid = coverage limit − deductible.
2. Damage: the customer bears **KRW 50,000** per incident. Amount actually paid = covered repair cost − deductible.

## Article 4 (Loss and the 180-day retention)

After a loss report, the period the line is suspended is excluded from the 180-day retention (GUIDE-SUB-02 §1), up to 30 days.

## Article 5 (Replacement devices and subsidies)

No public subsidy or switch bonus is newly paid for a device received as an insurance replacement.
