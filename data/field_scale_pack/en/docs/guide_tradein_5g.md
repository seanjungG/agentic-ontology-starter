# Device trade-in reward guide — 5G smartphone line

> Document no.: GUIDE-TRADEIN-5G · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> Amounts, rates and periods in this document may be changed by later policy amendment notices (NOTICE). A notice prevails over this document.

## Article 1 (Definition)

The trade-in reward pays **a share of the device list price at activation** when a 5G smartphone line device is returned after a period of use.

## Article 2 (Requirements)

1. The customer must join the programme at activation; joining later is not possible.
2. The line must be kept active until the return.

## Article 3 (Reward rate)

1. Returned after **12 months or more and less than 24 months** of use: **50%** of the list price
2. Returned after **24 months or more** of use: **30%** of the list price
3. No reward for a return before 12 months of use.

## Article 4 (Device grade)

1. Grade A (normal): the Article 3 rate applies as it is.
2. Grade B (cosmetic damage): only **80%** of the Article 3 reward is paid.
3. Grade C (malfunction): no reward.

## Article 5 (Applicable rate)

The rate in force on the **return date** applies.
