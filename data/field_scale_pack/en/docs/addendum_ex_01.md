# Subsidy exceptions addendum (common)

> Document no.: ADDENDUM-EX-01 · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> This addendum prevails over the corresponding articles of the guides.

## Addendum Article 1 (Designated island and mountain stores)

Notwithstanding Article 3(1) of each line's switch bonus guide, port-in customers who activate at a designated island or mountain store (store code starting with `ISL-`) may receive a switch bonus of up to **120%** of the limit of their tier.

## Addendum Article 2 (Corporate lines)

Notwithstanding Article 1 of each line's switch bonus guide, lines in a corporate name are **excluded** from the switch bonus even when ported in. Corporate lines cannot join a family bundle either. This article prevails over Addendum Article 1.

## Addendum Article 3 (Lines in a minor's name)

A ported-in line activated in the name of a person under 19 may receive a switch bonus of up to **50%** of the limit of its tier, only with a legal guardian's consent form. Without the form nothing is paid.

## Addendum Article 4 (Discharged service members)

Customers who port in within 6 months of military discharge are **exempt** from the minimum-use requirement of Article 2(3) of each line's switch bonus guide.

## Addendum Article 5 (Disaster areas)

Customers whose address is in an area declared a special disaster zone are **exempt** from switch bonus clawback for 6 months from the declaration.

## Addendum Article 6 (Overlapping exceptions)

1. When Addendum Articles 1 and 3 both apply, the two rates are multiplied (limit × 120% × 50%).
2. Exception rates apply to the limit **before** any time-limited promotion add-on, and a promotion add-on cannot be combined with an exception.
3. In this addendum 'limit' means the limit in force on the activation date (after amendment notices).
