# Policy amendment notice 2026-03

> Document no.: NOTICE-2026-03 · Issued: 2026-02-20 · Effective: 2026-03-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> The switch bonus applies to lines **activated** on or after the effective date, device insurance to **incidents** on or after it, and the trade-in reward to devices **returned** on or after it. Family bundle and loyalty discounts apply to every line from the bill of the month of the effective date, whatever the activation date. Earlier cases follow the rules in force at the time.

## Item 1 (Device trade-in reward — further adjustment)

The reward rate after 12 months amended by NOTICE-2026-02 item 5 is amended again **from 45% to 55%**.

## Item 2 (Loyalty discount — adjustment)

The discount from 3 years in GUIDE-LOYAL-TEEN Article 2 is amended **from KRW 1,000 to KRW 500**.

## Item 3 (Spring switch promotion — time-limited)

Port-in customers of the 5G smartphone line who activate between 2026-03-01 and 2026-04-30 on a HIGH-tier plan may receive **KRW 50,000 on top of** the limit then in force under GUIDE-SWITCH-5G Article 3(1). It does not apply to the MID tier.

## Item 4 (Family bundle discount — adjustment)

The 3-line discount in GUIDE-FAMILY-TEEN Article 2 is amended **from KRW 3,500 to KRW 4,500**.

## Item 5 (Device insurance — adjustment)

The loss coverage rate in GUIDE-INSUR-LTE Article 2(1) is amended **from 75% to 70%**.

## Item 6 (Port-in switch bonus — adjustment)

The minimum time with the previous carrier in GUIDE-SWITCH-TEEN Article 2(3) is amended **from 90 days to 120 days**.

## Item 7 (Loyalty discount — further adjustment)

The discount from 5 years amended by NOTICE-2026-02 item 6 is amended again **from KRW 1,500 to KRW 2,000**.

## Item 8 (Device insurance — adjustment)

The damage coverage cap in GUIDE-INSUR-TAB Article 2(2) is amended **from KRW 200,000 to KRW 220,000**.

## Item 9 (Unchanged)

NOTICE-2026-02 item 1, NOTICE-2026-02 item 7 remain as they are.
