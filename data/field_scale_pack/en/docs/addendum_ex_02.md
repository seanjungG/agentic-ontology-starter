# Subsidy exceptions addendum (by product line)

> Document no.: ADDENDUM-EX-02 · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> This addendum prevails over ADDENDUM-EX-01 and the guides.

## Addendum Article 1 (Minors on the teen & kids line)

The 50% restriction of ADDENDUM-EX-01 Addendum Article 3 does not apply to the teen & kids line (GUIDE-SWITCH-TEEN). On the teen & kids line a line in a minor's name may receive up to 100% of the limit of its tier. The legal guardian's consent form is still required.

## Addendum Article 2 (Island and mountain stores on the tablet & wearable line)

The 120% uplift of ADDENDUM-EX-01 Addendum Article 1 does not apply to the tablet & wearable line (GUIDE-SWITCH-TAB).

## Addendum Article 3 (Old devices on the LTE smartphone line)

LTE smartphone line devices more than 36 months past their release date are excluded from the trade-in reward of GUIDE-TRADEIN-LTE.

## Addendum Article 4 (Loss deductible for customers aged 65 or over)

For customers aged 65 or over, the loss deductible rate of Article 3(1) of each line's device insurance guide is **reduced by 5 percentage points**. The rate in force on the incident date (after amendment notices) is the basis.
