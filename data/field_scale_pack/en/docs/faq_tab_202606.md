**Q1. How much MNP bonus can an H-tier second device port-in get?**
A. Up to KRW 110,000 at the moment.

**Q2. And on the M-tier?**
A. Up to KRW 60,000.

**Q3. How long must they have been with the old carrier?**
A. At least 60 days.

**Q4. What is the Fam pack discount for 3 lines?**
A. KRW 1,500 per line per month. 2 lines: KRW 1,000; 4 or more: KRW 2,000.

**Q5. What does a PhoneCare customer get for a lost device?**
A. The cover is 70% of the list price, of which 20% is the customer's excess.

**Q6. And a cracked screen?**
A. Repair is covered up to KRW 220,000 per incident and the excess is KRW 30,000.

**Q7. How much does the Return plan pay after one year?**
A. 40% of the list price; 25% after two years.

**Q8. How much is the Long-run discount in year 5?**
A. KRW 2,000 per month. Year 3: KRW 500; year 10: KRW 2,500.

**Q9. Until when can insurance be taken out?**
A. Within 14 days of activation.

**Q10. Can a selective-discount customer get the MNP bonus?**
A. Yes, but not the public subsidy or the retailer additional subsidy.
