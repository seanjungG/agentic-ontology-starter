# Policy amendment notice 2026-02

> Document no.: NOTICE-2026-02 · Issued: 2026-01-23 · Effective: 2026-02-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> The switch bonus applies to lines **activated** on or after the effective date, device insurance to **incidents** on or after it, and the trade-in reward to devices **returned** on or after it. Family bundle and loyalty discounts apply to every line from the bill of the month of the effective date, whatever the activation date. Earlier cases follow the rules in force at the time.

## Item 1 (Loyalty discount — adjustment)

The discount from 10 years in GUIDE-LOYAL-TAB Article 2 is amended **from KRW 2,000 to KRW 2,500**.

## Item 2 (Port-in switch bonus — adjustment)

The MID-tier limit in GUIDE-SWITCH-LTE Article 3(1) is amended **from KRW 150,000 to KRW 170,000**.

## Item 3 (Family bundle discount — adjustment)

The 2-line discount in GUIDE-FAMILY-TAB Article 2 is amended **from KRW 1,000 to KRW 500**.

## Item 4 (Family bundle discount — adjustment)

The 4-or-more-line discount in GUIDE-FAMILY-TEEN Article 2 is amended **from KRW 5,000 to KRW 5,500**.

## Item 5 (Device trade-in reward — adjustment)

The reward rate after 12 months in GUIDE-TRADEIN-5G Article 3(1) is amended **from 50% to 45%**.

## Item 6 (Loyalty discount — adjustment)

The discount from 5 years in GUIDE-LOYAL-TAB Article 2 is amended **from KRW 1,000 to KRW 1,500**.

## Item 7 (Loyalty discount — adjustment)

The discount from 3 years in GUIDE-LOYAL-LTE Article 2 is amended **from KRW 1,500 to KRW 2,000**.
