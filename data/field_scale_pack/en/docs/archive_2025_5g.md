## 1. Port-in switch bonus (2025 rules)

| Plan tier | 2025 limit |

| HIGH tier | KRW 240,000 |

The minimum time with the previous carrier was 60 days. Cancelling within 90 days of activation meant clawback in full.

## 2. Family bundle discount (2025 rules)

2 lines KRW 2,500, 3 lines KRW 4,000, 4 or more KRW 5,500 (monthly discount per line). It could not be combined with the selective discount.

## 3. Device insurance (2025 rules)

Loss coverage was 70% of the list price and the loss deductible rate 20%. Damage was covered up to KRW 300,000 per incident with a deductible of KRW 40,000.

## 4. Device trade-in reward (2025 rules)

40% after 12 months of use, 25% after 24 months.

## 5. Loyalty discount (2025 rules)

From 3 years KRW 1,000, from 5 years KRW 3,000, from 10 years KRW 5,500 (monthly discount).
