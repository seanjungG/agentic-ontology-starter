# Port-in switch bonus guide — tablet & wearable line

> Document no.: GUIDE-SWITCH-TAB · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> Amounts, rates and periods in this document may be changed by later policy amendment notices (NOTICE). A notice prevails over this document.

## Article 1 (Definition and eligibility)

1. The **switch bonus** is paid, **separately** from the public subsidy, to customers who port in from another carrier and join a tablet & wearable line plan.
2. New subscriptions and device changes are not eligible.
3. It is paid once at activation, by deducting it from the device list price.

## Article 2 (Requirements)

All of the following must be met.

1. Activation by number port-in
2. A plan at or above the MID tier of the tablet & wearable line (monthly fee KRW 15,000)
3. At least **60 days** of use of the number with the previous carrier
4. No switch bonus received from us in the last 12 months

## Article 3 (Limits)

1. The switch bonus limit depends on the plan tier.

| Plan tier | Tier threshold (monthly fee) | Switch bonus limit |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| HIGH tier | KRW 30,000 or more | KRW 100,000 |
| MID tier | KRW 15,000 or more, below KRW 30,000 | KRW 60,000 |

2. No switch bonus is paid for plans below the MID tier.
3. The switch bonus is not part of the total subsidy (public subsidy + retailer additional subsidy). Net purchase price = device list price − total subsidy − switch bonus.

## Article 4 (Clawback)

1. If a customer who received the switch bonus cancels the line or moves below the MID tier within 180 days of activation, the switch bonus is clawed back **in full**.
2. There is no clawback after 180 days.
3. The switch bonus clawback is calculated separately from the downgrade settlement (GUIDE-SUB-02 §3) and the two are added.

## Article 5 (Selective discount)

Port-in customers who activate with the 25% selective discount can also receive the switch bonus if Article 2 is met. They still receive no public subsidy and no retailer additional subsidy.
