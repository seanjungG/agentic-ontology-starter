# Loyalty discount guide — 5G smartphone line

> Document no.: GUIDE-LOYAL-5G · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> Amounts, rates and periods in this document may be changed by later policy amendment notices (NOTICE). A notice prevails over this document.

## Article 1 (Eligibility)

1. The loyalty discount lowers the monthly fee of customers who have kept a 5G smartphone line for a long time.
2. Tenure is counted from the first activation of the line. A customer who ported out and came back starts again from the return date.

## Article 2 (Discount)

The following amount is discounted every month according to tenure.

| Tenure | Monthly discount |
| :--- | ---: |
| 3 years or more, under 5 | KRW 2,000 |
| 5 years or more, under 10 | KRW 4,000 |
| 10 years or more | KRW 7,000 |

## Article 3 (Other discounts)

1. The loyalty discount can be combined with the family bundle discount and the selective discount.
2. With the selective discount: monthly payment = monthly fee × 0.75 − loyalty discount (minus the family bundle discount as well, if any).

## Article 4 (When it applies)

When the tenure band changes, the discount of the new band applies from the next monthly bill.
