# Family bundle discount guide — teen & kids line

> Document no.: GUIDE-FAMILY-TEEN · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Audience: store and contact-centre agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document
> Amounts, rates and periods in this document may be changed by later policy amendment notices (NOTICE). A notice prevails over this document.

## Article 1 (Eligible lines)

1. The family bundle groups **2 to 5 lines** of one family on the teen & kids line and discounts each line every month.
2. Family relationship is checked with a resident register or family certificate issued within the last 3 months.
3. When lines of different product lines are bundled together, each line gets **the discount of its own line's guide**, and the line count is the count of the whole bundle.

## Article 2 (Discount)

Each line is discounted every month by the following amount, **per line**, according to the number of bundled lines.

| Bundled lines | Monthly discount per line |
| :--- | ---: |
| 2 lines | KRW 2,000 |
| 3 lines | KRW 3,500 |
| 4 or more lines | KRW 5,000 |

## Article 3 (Other discounts)

1. The family bundle discount can be combined with the selective discount (25% of the monthly fee).
2. Monthly payment of a line with both = monthly fee × 0.75 − monthly discount per line.
3. It can also be combined with the loyalty discount; both discounts are then deducted.

## Article 4 (Change in line count)

1. When a member leaves and the count drops, the discount of the smaller count applies from the next monthly bill.
2. Leaving or ending the bundle incurs no penalty and no discount refund.

## Article 5 (Lines below the MID tier)

A line on a plan below the MID tier (monthly fee KRW 29,000) still counts toward the bundle size, but that line itself gets no discount.
