# Device Subsidy Policy Guide (for support agents)

> Document no.: GUIDE-SUB-01 · Effective date: 2026-01-01 · Audience: store and customer-center support agents · De-identified synthetic document for hands-on training

## 1. Purpose and scope

This guide sets the standards support agents must follow when explaining device subsidy terms to customers who are activating a new phone or switching devices.
The subsidy amounts themselves come from the system's 'Subsidy policy table by device and plan'; this document covers the rules for interpreting and calculating those amounts.

## 2. Types of subsidy

1. **Public subsidy**: the base subsidy that the carrier publishes in advance for each device and plan combination. It is deducted once, at activation, directly from the device's retail price.
2. **Retailer additional subsidy**: an extra amount that an agency or store may give on top of the public subsidy. It may be paid only **up to 15% of the public subsidy**.
3. The public subsidy and the retailer additional subsidy combined are called the **total subsidy**.

## 3. Net purchase price calculation

- Net purchase price (installment principal) = device retail price − total subsidy
- Example: for a device with a retail price of KRW 1,000,000, a customer who receives a public subsidy of KRW 300,000 and a retailer additional subsidy of KRW 45,000 gets a total subsidy of KRW 345,000 and a net purchase price of KRW 655,000.
- Retail prices and subsidies are all stated in Korean won (KRW), VAT included.

## 4. 25% discount contract (bill discount instead of subsidy)

1. Instead of the public subsidy, a customer can choose the **25% discount contract (selective discount)**. It takes **25%** off the plan's monthly fee every month.
2. A customer who chooses the 25% discount contract does not receive the public subsidy or the retailer additional subsidy.
3. Explain the contract term on a 24-month basis.

## 5. 24-month total cost comparison

Support agents calculate both options, recommend the one with the lower 24-month total cost, and explain the difference to the customer.

- Public subsidy option: 24-month total cost = (retail price − total subsidy) + monthly fee × 24
- 25% discount contract option: 24-month total cost = retail price + monthly fee × 0.75 × 24
- The difference between the two total costs equals the difference between the 'total subsidy' and the '24-month total discount of the 25% discount contract (monthly fee × 0.25 × 24)'.
