# Store and Contact Center Subsidy Operations Manual

> Document no.: MANUAL-OPS-01 · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Audience: store and contact-center agents · Synthetic, de-identified training document · Amount and period rules follow GUIDE-SUB-01 to 03

## Part 1. Activation counselling

### Chapter 1. Explaining the subscription options

#### Section 1. Confirming the subscription option

1. Before activation, the agent first confirms whether the customer chooses the public subsidy option or the selective discount option.
2. The two options cannot be combined. A customer who chooses the selective discount receives neither the public subsidy nor the retailer additional subsidy.
3. The selective discount takes 25% off the monthly plan fee every month, and the contract period is explained on a 24-month basis.

#### Section 2. 24-month total cost comparison

1. The agent calculates the 24-month total cost of both options, recommends the cheaper one and explains the difference (GUIDE-SUB-01 §5).
2. The comparison shows the device's factory price, the total subsidy, the plan's monthly fee and the 24-month total cost of each option.

### Chapter 2. Paying subsidies

#### Section 1. Checking the payment limit

1. The retailer additional subsidy may be paid only within 15% of the public subsidy. Amounts above the limit are neither promised nor paid.
2. The total subsidy is the public subsidy plus the retailer additional subsidy.

#### Section 2. Explaining the net purchase price

1. The net purchase price (installment principal) is the device's factory price minus the total subsidy.
2. All amounts are explained in KRW, including VAT.

## Part 2. Post-activation changes

### Chapter 1. Accepting plan changes

#### Section 1. Checks before accepting

1. The agent first checks the customer's subscription option, the days elapsed since activation, and the monthly fee and plan category of the new plan.
2. If a public-subsidy customer wants to move below the baseline plan tier (KRW 45,000 per month for 5G activations) within 180 days, the agent first explains that a clawback applies.

#### Section 2. Explaining and processing the clawback

1. The agent calculates the clawback with the formula in GUIDE-SUB-02 §3, explains the amount and processes the plan change once the customer agrees.
2. If 180 days or more have elapsed, or the change is between plans at or above the baseline tier, the change is processed without a clawback.

### Chapter 2. Exemptions and pauses

#### Section 1. Processing the senior/welfare exemption

1. If the welfare status is SENIOR_EXEMPT and the new plan's category is SENIOR_WELFARE, the clawback is fully waived (KRW 0) and the change is processed.
2. If either condition is not met, the general clawback rule (GUIDE-SUB-02) applies.

#### Section 2. Processing the military service pause

1. After confirming the welfare status MILITARY_PAUSE_ELIGIBLE, the agent suspends the line. During the pause the 180-day retention period stops counting.
2. When the line is used again after discharge, the remaining retention period continues.
