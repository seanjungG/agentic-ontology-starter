## Domain at a glance
1. A customer who receives a public subsidy must keep a plan at or above the baseline for a fixed period after activation.
2. Downgrading below the baseline within that period costs a difference penalty proportional to the remaining period.
3. Senior and welfare customers are exempt, and a military enlistment hold pauses the commitment clock.
4. Contract comparison computes the 24-month total cost of both the public subsidy and the selective contract and recommends the cheaper one.

The exact periods, amounts and rates are on [rules].
